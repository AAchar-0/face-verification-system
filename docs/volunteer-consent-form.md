## Why you are being asked

I am testing a program that checks whether a person is who they say they are by comparing their face to a stored template. I need a few volunteers to try it so that I can measure how well it works.

## What will happen

1. You will look at a computer camera for a short session.
2. The program will turn your face into a list of 128 numbers (a "template"). **No photo or video is saved.**
3. The template is encrypted and stored only on my computer. It is never uploaded, emailed or published.
4. You will then try to verify yourself a number of times, and the program will say "Verified" or "Not verified".

## How your data is used and kept

- Your template is used only for this test.
- It is stored under a code name (for example `friend_1`), not under your name.
- I will delete it at the end of the test session, and in any case within 7 days.
- Results are reported only as anonymous totals, for example "3 volunteers, 20 attempts each". Your face will never appear in screenshots, recordings or the project's published files.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary, and you can say no without giving a reason.
- You can stop at any time and ask me to delete your template immediately.
- You can ask me to confirm in writing that it has been deleted.
- You must be 18 or older to take part.
